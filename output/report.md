## Method

Every tag (active, endpoint, model, fluoride-free arm) is a pattern match on the registry text; the matching snippet is saved in `trials.csv` (`agent_evidence`) so each call can be checked. Industry = lead sponsor class INDUSTRY or a known oral-care brand as lead or collaborator.
